Do I need editing skills?

No. The crew cuts, captions and checks the clips. You approve them.

What do I need?

A paid Claude plan and the accounts you want to post to. The crew prepares each channel sign-up; you tap create, because the platforms require the account owner to do that.

Will it make me money?

We don't promise views or income. Clip Crew does the daily work of running a clip channel. What the channel earns depends on the platforms, your niche and your consistency.

Where do the clips come from?

From footage you record or have the right to use. Follow each platform's rules and the game publisher's content policy.
